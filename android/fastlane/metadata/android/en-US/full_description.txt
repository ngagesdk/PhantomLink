PhantomLink brings the multiplayer modes of classic N-Gage games online. The games were made for Bluetooth play in the same room; with PhantomLink, your N-Gage connects to your phone over Bluetooth and the phone forwards the game over the Internet to a PhantomLink server, where you meet other players.

This app turns your Android phone into the bridge. It does the same job as the PhantomLink ESP32 board or the PC bridge, and runs exactly the same bridge engine.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Install PhantomLink on your N-Gage (the replacement GameComms library).
2. Tap Start in this app.
3. On the N-Gage, start a multiplayer game and select your phone in the Bluetooth list. If asked for a PIN, enter 1234.
That's it. The game thinks it is talking to another N-Gage nearby.

FEATURES
• Finds a PhantomLink server on your local network automatically, or connects to any server address you enter
• Up to 7 N-Gages at the same time through one phone
• Answers the N-Gage's pairing PIN for you
• Rides out short Wi-Fi or mobile data drops without ending the game
• Keeps running in the background with the screen off
• Live status of every connected N-Gage and a detailed log you can share
• Light and dark theme
• No account, no ads, no tracking

REQUIREMENTS
• An N-Gage (Classic or QD) with the PhantomLink GameComms library installed
• A PhantomLink server: run your own (free, open source) or use one shared by your community
• A game that uses the standard N-Gage Bluetooth multiplayer library. See the compatibility list on the project page.
